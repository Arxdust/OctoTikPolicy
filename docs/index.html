OctoTik Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

OctoTik is a MikroTik Hotspot management tool for Android, iOS and Windows. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: OctoTik does not run any servers and does not collect your data.

1.Information We Collect

OctoTik does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers or to any third parties. We operate no backend servers. All communication happens locally and directly between your device and your own MikroTik router.

2.Local Storage

The following data is stored only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Router connection details (host, port, username);
  • Your router password, kept in the platform's secure storage;
  • App preferences such as theme and tab layout.

You can remove all of this data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

3.Router Operations

Any configuration changes you make through OctoTik (managing hotspot users, disconnecting active sessions, reading interface statistics) are applied directly on your own MikroTik router. This data stays on your router and is not shared with us or anyone else.

4.Permissions

OctoTik requests only the permissions it needs to function:

  • Internet / local network access — to communicate with your router.

5.Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. There are no embedded SDKs that send your data elsewhere.

6.Children's Privacy

OctoTik is a network administration tool intended for professional and technical users. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7.Data Security

Because no data is sent to us, there is no central database that could be breached. Credentials are stored using the secure storage facilities provided by your operating system.

8.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, with an updated effective date at the top.

9.Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us via Telegram: @SeafarerDOC_Support.
