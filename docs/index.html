Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

OctoTik OctoClient

This Privacy Policy applies to OctoTik and OctoClient (together, "the apps") — MikroTik management tools for Android, iOS and Windows. It explains how the apps handle your information. In short: the apps do not run any servers and do not collect your data. The same terms apply to each app individually.

1.Information We Collect

The apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers or to any third parties. We operate no backend servers. All communication happens locally and directly between your device and your own MikroTik router.

2.Local Storage

The following data is stored only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Router connection details (host, port, username);
  • Your router password, kept in the platform's secure storage;
  • App preferences such as theme and tab layout.

You can remove all of this data at any time by clearing an app's data or uninstalling the app.

3.Router Operations

Any configuration changes you make through the apps (managing hotspot users, disconnecting active sessions, reading interface statistics) are applied directly on your own MikroTik router. This data stays on your router and is not shared with us or anyone else.

4.Permissions

The apps request only the permissions they need to function:

  • Internet / local network access — to communicate with your router.

5.Third-Party Services

The apps do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. There are no embedded SDKs that send your data elsewhere.

6.Children's Privacy

The apps are network administration tools intended for professional and technical users. They are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect information from them.

7.Data Security

Because no data is sent to us, there is no central database that could be breached. Credentials are stored using the secure storage facilities provided by your operating system.

8.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, with an updated effective date at the top.

9.Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us via Telegram: @SeafarerDOC_Support.
